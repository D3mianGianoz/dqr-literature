# Goldstein (2023) — *Special Issue on Unsupervised Anomaly Detection*

Editorial overview of 12 papers in an *Applied Sciences* special issue. The
selection covers classical and deep-learning methods, semi-supervised training
on normal examples, contextual-to-point anomaly formulations, and methods for
explaining detections. The editorial is a high-level overview; claims about
individual methods should be checked against the underlying papers.
